# ADR-0062: `core.operator` — Finite-Dimensional $C^*$-Algebras, $\sigma C^*$-Algebras, and Exact Choi–PSD $\sigma$CP-Instruments

- **Status:** Accepted — Wave 1 implementation in `core.operator`
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Scope:** Exact algebraic $C^*$-algebras over $\mathbb{Q}[i]$, commutative & noncommutative finite/effective $\sigma C^*$-algebras, exact Choi–Jamiołkowski complete-positivity certification via `core.exact_psd`, Kraus representations, Stinespring dilations, and compositional $\sigma$CP-instruments grounded in Loregian & Lorenzin (`arXiv:2611.32363`) and Fritz & Lorenzin (`doi:10.1016/j.aim.2026.110793`).
- **Depends on:** `core.exact_linalg`, `core.exact_psd`, `core.sheaf.site`, `core.category` (`ADR-0036`/`ADR-0054`), `core.monoidal` (`ADR-0041`), `core.sblc` (`ADR-0017`).

## Context

Classical measure-theoretic probability and quantum information traditionally diverge into incompatible abstractions:
- Ordinary commutative $C^*$-algebras $C(X)$ only encode *continuous* Markov kernels (Furber–Jacobs), which cannot express general Bayesian disintegrations.
- Von Neumann algebras quotient out null sets and lose normality under ordinary $C^*$-tensor products.
- Unitary diagram languages (`core.monoidal.zx`) encode linear/unitary structure rather than probabilistic channels or measurement instruments.

Loregian & Lorenzin (`arXiv:2611.32363`, 2026) prove that:
1. The forgetful functor $\sigma C^*\mathbf{Alg}_\sigma \to C^*\mathbf{Alg}$ is **monadic** (Theorem 3.3), with monad constructing the Pedersen–Baire envelope.
2. Commutative $\sigma C^*$-algebras are equivalent to **Boolean $\sigma$-algebras** via $\Sigma \mapsto \mathscr{L}_{\mathrm{abs}}^\infty(\Sigma)$ (point-free measurable spaces).
3. Probabilistic morphisms ($\sigma$-cpu maps) arise via a **noncommutative Giry comonad** (Section 4) over a symmetric monoidal maximal tensor product $\boxtimes$ (Section 6).
4. **$\sigma$CP-instruments** $p \colon \Sigma \to \sigma\mathrm{CP}(A, B)$ correspond bijectively to $\sigma$-cpu maps $\mathscr{L}_{\mathrm{abs}}^\infty(\Sigma) \boxtimes A \to B$ (Theorem 7.6), yielding automatic sequential composition $p' \circ p \colon \Sigma \otimes \Sigma' \to \sigma\mathrm{CP}(A, C)$ (Proposition 7.7).

## Decision (Operational Correspondence)

1. **Exact $\mathbb{Q}[i]$ Matrix & Direct-Sum $C^*$-Algebras (`core.operator.cstar`).**
   Every element of $M_n(\mathbb{Q}[i])$ is stored as an exact pair `(re . im)` of exact rational matrices built with `(vector ...)`, never floating-point `#(...)` literals. Complex arithmetic, involution $a^* = (\mathrm{Re}\,a)^T - i(\mathrm{Im}\,a)^T$, product $ab$, commutator $[a, b] = ab - ba$, trace $\mathrm{Tr}(a)$, Hilbert–Schmidt inner product $\langle a, b \rangle = \mathrm{Tr}(a^* b)$, and Hermitian/unitary/projection/commutativity predicates are **exact over $\mathbb{Q}[i]$**.
   Hermitian positive semidefiniteness of $H = H^* \in M_n(\mathbb{Q}[i])$ is certified **without floating-point error** by lifting $H$ to its $2n \times 2n$ real symmetric block representation
   $$R(H) = \begin{pmatrix} \mathrm{Re}\,H & -\mathrm{Im}\,H \\ \mathrm{Im}\,H & \mathrm{Re}\,H \end{pmatrix}$$
   and invoking `exact-psd-factor` from `core.exact_psd`: PSD succeeds with exact factor $(B, D)$, while indefiniteness returns an **exact rational witness vector** $v = (x, y) \in \mathbb{Q}^{2n}$ with $\langle \psi | H | \psi \rangle < 0$.

2. **Point-Free Boolean $\sigma$-Algebras & $\sigma C^*$-Tensor Products (`core.operator.sigma_cstar`).**
   Finite Boolean $\sigma$-algebras $\Sigma$ generated by atoms $\{e_1, \dots, e_k\}$ embed functorially into diagonal commutative $\sigma C^*$-algebras $\mathscr{L}_{\mathrm{abs}}^\infty(\Sigma) \cong \mathbb{Q}[i]^k$. The tensor product $\mathscr{L}_{\mathrm{abs}}^\infty(\Sigma) \boxtimes M_n(\mathbb{Q}[i])$ is realized explicitly as the block-diagonal algebra $\bigoplus_{i=1}^k M_n(\mathbb{Q}[i])$, preserving the exact universal property for commuting images and $\sigma$-normal order convergence on bounded monotone sequences.

3. **Exact Choi–PSD Certified $\sigma$CP-Instruments (`core.operator.cp_instrument`).**
   Any linear superoperator $\Phi \colon M_n(\mathbb{Q}[i]) \to M_m(\mathbb{Q}[i])$ is audited by constructing its exact Choi–Jamiołkowski matrix
   $$C_\Phi = \sum_{i,j=0}^{n-1} E_{ij} \otimes \Phi(E_{ij}) \in M_{nm}(\mathbb{Q}[i])$$
   and certifying $C_\Phi \succeq 0$ via `exact-psd-factor`. Non-CP maps (such as the matrix transpose $\Phi(X) = X^T$) are **rejected with an exact negative witness** rather than approximated. Both Heisenberg-picture $\sigma$-cpu maps ($\Phi(I) = I$) and Schrödinger-picture CPTP duals ($\mathrm{Tr}(\Phi(X)) = \mathrm{Tr}(X)$) are supported, along with Kraus evaluation $\Phi(X) = \sum_k K_k X K_k^*$ and Heisenberg dual $\Phi^*(A) = \sum_k K_k^* A K_k$.
   A **$\sigma$CP-instrument** $p \colon \Sigma \to \sigma\mathrm{CP}(A, B)$ enforces:
   - Complete positivity of every outcome branch $p(e_i)$ (exact Choi–PSD),
   - $\sigma$-additivity $p(U \vee V) = p(U) + p(V)$ for disjoint $U \wedge V = \bot$,
   - Unitality $p(\top)(I) = I$ (Heisenberg) or trace-preservation $\sum_i \mathrm{Tr}(p(e_i)(\rho)) = \mathrm{Tr}(\rho)$ (Schrödinger),
   - Exact Theorem 7.6 isomorphism to a single $\sigma$-cpu map $\mathscr{L}_{\mathrm{abs}}^\infty(\Sigma) \boxtimes A \to B$, and
   - Exact Proposition 7.7 sequential composition $p' \circ p \colon \Sigma \otimes \Sigma' \to \sigma\mathrm{CP}(A, C)$.

4. **Strict Separation from `core.monoidal.zx`.**
   ZX diagrams generate unitary/linear operators; probabilistic state updates and measurements enter exclusively through `core.operator.cp_instrument`, where Choi–PSD and unitality/trace-preservation are enforced before any quantum-probabilistic claim is admitted.

## Scope & Honest Limitations

Wave 1 implements exact finite-dimensional $C^*$-algebras ($M_n(\mathbb{Q}[i])$ and direct sums), finite Boolean $\sigma$-algebras $\mathscr{L}_{\mathrm{abs}}^\infty(\Sigma)$, exact Choi–PSD certification, Kraus/Heisenberg duality, and Theorem 7.6 / Proposition 7.7 $\sigma$CP-instrument composition. Infinite-dimensional separable Hilbert spaces, unbounded operators, and certified ball-arithmetic spectral enclosures belong to the next slice (`core.analysis.functional` / `core.analysis.spectral`).
